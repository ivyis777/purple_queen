Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about working with Purple Queen Interiors.

Our complete home interior packages typically start from ₹2.85 Lakhs for a 1BHK, ₹4.85 Lakhs for a 2BHK, and ₹7.50 Lakhs for a 3BHK. The exact cost depends on your choice of materials (acrylic vs laminate vs veneer) and the scope of work (modular woodwork, civil changes, false ceiling, and lighting).

Our 10-year warranty covers all modular woodwork against bending, delamination, termite infestation, and manufacturing defects. All hardware components (hinges, drawer channels, lift-up systems) come with manufacturer warranties up to 10 years or lifetime.

Because 90% of our cabinetry, panels, and modules are manufactured in our automated factory while civil and electrical work is underway on site, on-site assembly only takes 10-15 days. If we exceed the promised 45 days handover date, we pay you a penalty rent of ₹1,000 per day.
